  • Shifted poses are much better on Isaac. Replanning every 4 steps removed the privileged policy's grasp misses entirely (grasp success 100%).
  • Recovery is the gap. The Isaac expert itself recovers only 81.7% of knocks (MuJoCo's: 96%), which caps what imitation can learn.
  • DAgger teaches less on Isaac. Isaac has no simulator snapshot, so the expert can only correct by taking over: 403 corrections in one round, against a label at every replan point on MuJoCo. One round added +5 pp recovery here against +17 pp on MuJoCo.
  • Most recovery failures are missed re-grasps. After the knock the gripper closes more than 3 cm from its corner, so the grasp never engages: 50 of 91 privileged failures, 115 of 138 sensor-only failures.

Open items

What this does not settle yet

  • Recovery (54.5% privileged, 31.0% sensor-only). Next options: more DAgger rounds with more expert takeovers (about 1.5 h per round), or a better expert re-grasp.
  • The weld grasp is a stand-in for LeHome's friction grasp, whose track is paused. These numbers hold for the weld profile only.
  • A parallel branch (feat/isaac-half-fold) ports the same task with an anchor grasp and BC only (9/10 clean, n = 10). The two need reconciling before either lands on main.
