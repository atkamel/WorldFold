# Half-fold on Isaac Sim — 2026-10-06 (Phase W, W5: the imitation pipeline retrained on Isaac)

**Dated:** 6 Oct 2026 · **Branch:** `feature/isaac-imitation` · **Results as of:** `{{COMMIT}}`
· **Sim:** Isaac Sim 5.1 (LeHome stack from `origin/main`, PR #15), weld grasp profile (`isaac_weld`)
· **Eval:** n = 200 per set, held-out MuJoCo eval seeds, deterministic inference, Wilson 95% intervals.
· **Previous report:** [2026-09-25-phase5b.md](2026-09-25-phase5b.md) (the same pipeline on MuJoCo)

This report covers the move from MuJoCo to the Isaac Sim cloth-fold environment that `origin/main` brought in. The
same pipeline was retrained from scratch there: expert demos → diffusion BC → DAgger → a camera-only student
distilled from the privileged policy. Every number is copied from [results.md](../results.md), the append-only
source of truth.

## Where it stands

There are three held-out benchmarks, the same seeds and definitions as the MuJoCo reports:
- **clean** (`id_easy`): nominal starts.
- **shifted** (`id_hard`): the cloth moved 2.5–4 cm.
- **recovery**: the policy is knocked off course by 8–15 random actions mid-episode.

| policy | inputs | clean | shifted | recovery |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Scripted expert (ceiling), n = 100 | sim state | 100/100 | 100/100 | 81/100 (pooled 147/180 = 81.7% [75.4, 86.6]) |
| **Privileged: diffusion + DAgger r1, replan 4** | 139-D state | **99.0%** [96.4, 99.7] | **97.5%** [94.3, 98.9] | 54.5% [47.6, 61.3] |
| **Sensor-only student, replan 2** | 3 cameras + 48 joint/gripper dims | **99.0%** [96.4, 99.7] | 82.5% [76.6, 87.1] | 31.0% [25.0, 37.7] |

**What the student sees.** The sensor-only student gets the main camera (128²), the two wrist cameras (64²), and the
48 proprio dims a real arm can measure:
- joint angles and velocities
- the gripper command
- the forward-kinematics gripper pose

It gets no cloth state, no corner positions and no grasp flags. It was trained on the privileged policy's actions
(the M5b.3 "round 0" recipe, which beat further distillation rounds on MuJoCo).

**Success detector.** The image-based success classifier agrees with the sim's success check on
**392/400 = 98.0% [96.1, 99.0]** of held-out episodes, with 0 false positives (MuJoCo: 92.6%).

## Against MuJoCo

The best MuJoCo policies at the same operating points (n = 200, same eval seeds):

| policy | clean | shifted | recovery |
|---|---|---|---|
| MuJoCo privileged (DAgger diffusion, replan 4) | 99.5% | 77.0% | **79.5%** |
| **Isaac privileged** (DAgger diffusion r1, replan 4) | 99.0% | **97.5%** | 54.5% |
| MuJoCo sensor-only (vision student, replan 2) | 94.0% | 91.0% | 41.0% |
| **Isaac sensor-only** (vision student, replan 2) | **99.0%** | 82.5% | 31.0% |

- **Shifted poses are much better on Isaac:** +20.5 pp for the privileged policy, with disjoint intervals.
- **Recovery is worse:** −25 pp. Two reasons:
  - The Isaac expert itself recovers only 81.7% of the time (MuJoCo's: 96%), which caps what imitation can learn.
  - Isaac has no sim snapshot for DAgger's look-ahead labels. Its DAgger uses expert *takeovers* instead, which
    gave 403 corrective labels in one round. That is far fewer than MuJoCo's look-ahead got, which labelled every
    replan point. One round added +5 pp recovery here against +17 pp on MuJoCo.
- **Half of the recovery failures are G1:** after the knock, the gripper closes more than 3 cm from its corner, so
  the weld never engages.
- **The sensor-only student matches its teacher on clean starts (99%) and beats MuJoCo's student there (+5 pp).**
  - It trails its teacher by 15 pp on shifted poses.
  - It trails by 23.5 pp on recovery, where 115 of its 138 failures are missed re-grasps. Without corner
    positions, finding a corner the knock moved is the hard part.

## How it was trained

| stage | data / setting | result (selection eval, n = 100, replan 8) |
|---|---|---|
| Expert demos | `isaac_v1_weld`: 400 seeds, 30% knocked; 384 successes kept (+16 failures for the detector); hash `468089355dd7`; 75 min at 2 processes × 8 envs | — |
| Diffusion BC, seed 0 / seed 1 | 30k steps each (M2.4 recipe), 36 min each | 100 / 85 / 44 · 98 / 73 / 38 |
| DAgger round 1 (from seed 0) | 128 student rollouts, expert takeovers p = 0.3 → 403 labels; 15k warm-start steps | 100 / 88 / 49 (+0.9 SE, kept) |
| Vision student | 30k steps, batch 256, every collected step relabelled by the DAgger policy; 19 min | final table above |
| Detector | 2k steps on `isaac_v1_weld` + failures | 98.0% agreement |

**Seed variance:** 12 pp on shifted between BC seeds 0 and 1 (MuJoCo: 8 pp). Treat single-seed differences under
about 10 pp as noise.

**Operating point:** replanning every 4 steps instead of 8 lifted the privileged policy's shifted score from 88% to
97.5%. Grasp success went to 100%: the aiming misses disappear.

## Open issues

1. **Recovery** (54.5% privileged) is the gap:
   - The cheapest next step is another DAgger round with more takeovers (p = 0.6, about +1.5 h).
   - The structural fix is a better expert re-grasp: W3b attempts A–D plateaued at 81.7%.
2. **The weld grasp is a stand-in.** The friction-grasp track (G, `feature/isaac-grasp`) is paused by decision. These
   results hold for the weld profile only.
3. **Heat budget** (laptop, 16 GB GPU): runs are capped at 2 Isaac processes × 4 envs, with a GPU thermal guard
   that pauses at 90 °C. The GPU peaked at 87 °C; there were no pauses.
