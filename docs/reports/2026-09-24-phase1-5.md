## The training pipeline, stage by stage

| # | stage | what it does | metric |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Scripted expert | DLS IK plus a phase machine; reads sim state; teacher for everything below | ceiling 100 / 97 / 96%; resync after handover 92-94% |
| 2 | Collect → `v1` | 400 seeds, 30% knocked off course. Streams to disk, resumable, deterministic. Failures go to `v1_failures` | 384 demos, 38,136 steps; clean 97.1%, perturbed 93.4%; hash `769dd372719c` |
| 3 | Behaviour cloning | chunk-MLP 8.65M: 2-step obs history → 16-action chunk, replan every 8; failures dropped; hashed seed split | 98.0 / 69.5 / 38.5%; diffusion head 99.5 / 80.0 / 51.0% |
| 4 | DAgger | the student drives and a **shadowing** expert labels every replan state; β 0.3 → 0.075; aggregated frozen versions; keep/stop in SE | 97.0 / 64.5 / 65.0% (recovery +26.5 pp over BC) |
| 5 | Offline RL (IQL) | 1,000-rollout harvest; chunk macro-actions; sparse success + clipped Δfold reward; expectile V, twin Q, AWR | harvest 81% success; IQL 94.0 / 67.5 / 45.5% (**worse**) |
| 6 | Render cameras | replay `v1` exactly, with per-episode visual DR → `v1_img` (main 96², wrists 64²) | 384 eps, replay obs diff 0.0 |
| 7 | Distill to sensor-only | vision student; the privileged DAgger policy relabels every visited step from the stored state; DAgger-style rounds | 97.5 / 90.5 / 31.5% |
| 8 | Vision success detector | CNN on the main camera → folded + fold score; sim labels are free | frame acc 94.9%, fold MAE 0.022, episode agreement 91.8% |
| 9 | Evaluate & demo | fixed held-out sets; mechanistic failure codes (G1 / F1 / S1 / M1); padded (deterministic) inference | demos 4/4 and 4/4 |

### Other measurements

- **Privileged-feature ablation (M2.3)**, clean / shifted / recovery: full 139-D 97.5 /
  72.5 / 37.0; proprio + corners 96.0 / 45.0 / 27.0; proprio only 92.0 / 48.5 / 26.0.
  Corner keypoints alone buy nothing under shift; relative cloth geometry does.
- **Harvest (M5.1)**: `harvest_v1`, 1,000 rollouts at σ = 0.1 → 811 success, 110 S1, 60 F1,
  19 G1 (`3b5d331bf404`).
- **IQL diagnosis (M5.3)**: the critic ranks states (V 0.94 on successful vs 0.20 on
  failed trajectories) but not actions (A = −0.010 ± 0.017 on both), so the update is BC
  over everything, stalls included.

## What broke, and the fix

- **Teacher labels contradicted the teacher.** Re-inferring phases from geometry and
  re-solving IK at every label disagreed with the expert on its own trajectory by up to
  0.57 per joint, and the first DAgger run regressed. Fixed with a shadowing teacher
  (`ScriptedTeacher.observe`); labels now match to < 0.02.
- **Evaluation wasn't repeatable.** The same checkpoint scored 145 and then 134/200:
  GPU outputs shift ~1e-6 with batch size and the cloth sim amplifies it. Fixed by
  padding inference to a fixed batch shape.
- **Collection wasn't repeatable.** The expert's IK rng carried over between episodes on
  a pooled worker. It's reseeded per episode, and identical runs are byte-identical.
- **Collection was all-or-nothing.** Episodes now stream to disk; `--resume` was tested
  by killing a real run.
